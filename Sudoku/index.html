How to Play Mini Sudoku

Each row, column, and 2×3 walled region must contain the numbers 1 through 6 with no repeats.

  • Notes Mode (N): Toggle handwritten candidate marks inside empty cells. Putting a single correct note in every remaining cell also wins the game!
  • Smart Hints (H): Uses a 5-tier deduction engine (Rule Violations, Single Restriction, Naked Single, and Hidden Single) with visual scrim highlighting.
  • Procedural Generator: Generates brand-new 6×6 puzzles with mirror or rotational symmetry and verifies a unique, human-deducible solution.
